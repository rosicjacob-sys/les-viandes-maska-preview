Cutting questions

Cutting questions

Is this an online order?

No. This composer prepares a request. No price, stock or lead time is confirmed automatically.

Can I request a different weight for each product?

Yes. Add each preparation separately, then add context in the request summary.

Is the calculated weight the weight of the piece I need to buy?

No. It is only the theoretical portion weight. An advisor must assess trimming, loss and yield.
