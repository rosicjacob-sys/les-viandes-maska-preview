Preview privacy

Your data stays in your hands.

This design version has no form server, analytics tool or advertising tracker.

01

Local storage

Your products, preparations and drafts are stored in this browser’s local storage under the maska.preview.v1 key. The site does not transmit them to Render or Maska.

02

Email

When you choose to open a draft, your email app takes over. Nothing is sent until you click Send in that app. Your email provider’s rules then apply.

03

Files and applications

The site does not upload resumes or other files. For a job application, attach your resume yourself in your email app and address it to cv@viandesmaska.com.

04

Cookies and analytics

No advertising cookies, analytics, tracking pixels or chat service are enabled in this preview.

05

Deletion

Use the button below to delete this preview’s selection and drafts from this browser. This does not delete emails you may already have sent.

06

Production version

A future transactional version must document its recipient, consent, retention, security and deletion before activation. This page describes only the current preview.

Delete this preview’s data

This immediately deletes locally stored products, preparations and drafts. It cannot be undone.
